# 🛡️ AI Powered Insurance Analytics System

An integrated Insurance AI platform combining Machine Learning and Generative AI for insurance analytics, risk assessment, fraud detection, customer segmentation, sentiment analysis, and policy assistance.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

The Insurance AI system provides a unified Streamlit dashboard containing Machine Learning and Generative AI applications for the insurance domain.

The platform combines predictive analytics, customer intelligence, fraud detection, sentiment analysis, and AI-powered policy services in a single application.

---

## 📊 Machine Learning Modules

### 1. Claim Prediction

Predicts whether an insurance claim is likely to occur based on customer and policy-related information.

### 2. Risk Classification

Classifies customers according to their insurance risk level.

### 3. Fraud Detection

Identifies potentially fraudulent insurance claims using a trained machine learning model.

### 4. Customer Segmentation

Segments insurance customers based on their characteristics and behaviour.

### 5. Sentiment Analysis

Analyzes customer reviews and classifies their sentiment.

---

## 🧠 Generative AI Modules

### 6. Policy Assistant

Provides assistance with insurance policy-related questions, including coverage, exclusions, and claim procedures.

### 7. Policy Translator

Translates insurance policy content into another language.

### 8. Policy Summarizer

Generates concise summaries of lengthy insurance policy content.

### 9. Insurance AI Chatbot

Provides an interactive AI assistant for insurance-related questions.
